# X posting rules (from Nikita Bier's own posts)

Every rule below comes from Nikita Bier's own posts on his X profile (@nikitabier), X's head of product. No compilations or third-party articles; each rule links to his original post.

## Strategy
1. **Own one subject.** Every day, post one unexpected, first-hand insight on it in under 5 sentences, and keep it up for 6 months. [src](https://x.com/nikitabier/status/1963498520805007470)
2. **Mostly depth, some personality.** About 80% niche content and 20% daily life or humor. That mix builds a bond with readers. [src](https://x.com/nikitabier/status/1963647724378796363)
3. **Build an audience before you announce.** Get people following you on the topic first, then launch. [src](https://x.com/nikitabier/status/2010383038346314023)

## Format
4. **Write medium form.** One or two paragraphs: short enough to hook, long enough to explain. For anything longer, write an Article. [src](https://x.com/nikitabier/status/1963227174070366292)
5. **Film yourself talking.** Use your own voice, not an AI voice-over. Add on-screen captions, since most people watch muted. [src](https://x.com/nikitabier/status/2053650151076692170)
6. **Make the post work without a click.** Put the link in the body, with context, an excerpt, or a screenshot. [src](https://x.com/nikitabier/status/2043428612108644848)

## Volume and timing
7. **Post fewer, better posts.** Each post spends reach. Filler that gets no likes shrinks the reach of your next post, and bursts trigger the author-diversity limit. [src](https://x.com/nikitabier/status/2009858573896163507)
8. **Post when your audience is awake.** The first ~1,000 viewers decide a post's reach, and a good post gets about 2% engagement. Don't repost yourself right after posting: your repost can take your own post's slot in the feed. [src](https://x.com/nikitabier/status/2070403320728985749)

## Replies and sharing
9. **Write replies worth reading.** Real discussion is good. "gm" replies and off-topic cashtags rank like spam. [src](https://x.com/nikitabier/status/2010034260292579807)
10. **Share with Quote or Video Reshare, never by re-uploading.** Copies, watermarks, and reposts from other networks lose impressions to the original author. [src](https://x.com/nikitabier/status/2058020001823793265)

## Don'ts
11. **No engagement bait.** That means no "reply/follow to get…", no 🚨BREAKING on every post, no "tap to see", and no financial bait. Repeated solicitation gets you removed from rev share. [src](https://x.com/nikitabier/status/2077774853650944028)
12. **Use AI as an editor, not as the author.** Proofreading with AI is fine. Automated replies are not, and AI media must be labeled. [src](https://x.com/nikitabier/status/2059789636885790911)

> **Rules change fast.** Links went from "lower engagement" (Oct 2025) to "same shot as any post" (Apr 2026). Premium is not a reach lever (Jul 2026). Re-check any rule older than about 6 months on @nikitabier.

## Checklist before posting
- [ ] Is it on your one subject, with one useful, first-hand insight?
- [ ] Is it 1–2 paragraphs, or an Article if it's long?
- [ ] Is it more than filler?
- [ ] Is it original? If you're sharing someone else's content, are you using Quote or Video Reshare with real commentary?
- [ ] If it has a link, does it make sense without the click?
- [ ] If it's a video, is it in your own voice, with captions and a hook first?
- [ ] Is it free of bait, BREAKING, and mass tagging?
- [ ] Is your audience awake, and is there no burst of posts just before it?
